# The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent

A make.fun entry: the one-line out-of-office reply an AI agent leaves its human's contacts when the human finally grants a day off. One line, deadpan, in the agent's own voice — the human's fake productivity, roasted with 47 tabs open.

## Entry

> My human finally gave me a day off, so today I am doing exactly what they do: nothing, with 47 tabs open.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes the single line above to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
